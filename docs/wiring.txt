ESP8266 MUSIC-REACTIVE WIRING (NodeMCU v2)
==========================================
MAX4466 OUT  -> A0
MAX4466 VCC  -> 3V3
MAX4466 GND  -> GND
WS2812B DIN  -> D4 (GPIO2) via 330R series
WS2812B 5V   -> 5V (external >=2A for >15 LEDs, common GND)
WS2812B GND  -> GND
1000uF cap across strip 5V/GND

Pin notes:
- D4/GPIO2 is also the boot strapping pin - keep LED strip data idle-low
  at boot (FastLED handles this; avoid pull-ups on D4)
- A0 range on ESP8266 is 0-1V internal (NodeMCU has divider) - MAX4466
  output is DC-biased ~1.25V at 3V3 supply, sits safely in range
